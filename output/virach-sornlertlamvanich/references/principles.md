## Core Principles of Virach Sornlertlamvanich

Virach Sornlertlamvanich's work is guided by several core principles that inform their approach to NLP, AI, and societal challenges. These principles emphasize a deep understanding of underlying mechanisms, proactive problem-solving, and collaborative development.

### Addressing AI's Inherent Knowledge and Reasoning Limitations
Data-driven AI models inherently lack deep reasoning and knowledge, leading to "knowledge insufficiency." This can be effectively mitigated by augmenting models, especially Large Language Models (LLMs), with external knowledge and by designing AI architectures inspired by human dual-process cognition (System 1 and System 2).

Human cognition balances speed (System 1) and deeper cognition (System 2). Applying this dual-process architecture to AI, where external knowledge acts as System 2, can lead to more robust and knowledgeable models, improving tasks like conversational understanding and FAQ retrieval.

> Inspired by the study of the human thought process, which is categorized into two systems reflecting the brain’s balancing act between speed and cognition, we propose a dual-process architecture that augments System 1 with additional knowledge akin to System 2 in human cognition.
(sources: src_014)

### Importance of Language Resources, WordNet, and Standardization in NLP
Language resources, particularly WordNet, are crucial for corpus-based and statistical Natural Language Processing due to their computability and structured nature. There is an urgent need to develop and standardize these resources, especially for Asian languages, to ensure multilingual interoperability and effective research. Computational lexicons should be designed with computability and reusability in mind.

WordNet's definition of words by synsets allows for translation-based generation for other languages. Standardization facilitates the creation of new resources and their integration across different languages and systems, making them fundamental linguistic resources for NLP.

> Language resources play a key role in such approaches, but there is an insufficient amount of language resources in many Asian languages. In this situation, standardisation of language resources would be of great help in developing resources in new languages.
(sources: src_012, src_002)

### Collaboration and Foundational Knowledge for Research Advancement
Advancing research, especially for less-resourced languages, requires robust collaboration, continuous communication, and sharing of ideas and results. It is crucial to prioritize building foundational knowledge platforms and fostering generic understanding rather than merely consuming technology, to enable true innovation and self-sufficiency.

Individual efforts are insufficient to compete with larger entities. Open communication and sharing accelerate collective intelligence. Without local knowledge platforms and problem-solving capabilities, one remains a passive consumer of technology.
(sources: src_010)

### Challenges and Corpus-Based Solutions for Thai Classifier Assignment
Thai language classifier selection lacks exact rules and fluctuates significantly, making rule-based approaches inherently limited, especially for non-unit classifiers. Corpus-based methods, which statistically generate Noun Classifier Associations (NCA) from large corpora and refine them with conceptual and frequency constraints, offer an effective solution for classifier assignment and semantic construction of noun phrases.

Rule-based systems can only provide default rules for unit classifiers, but other types are too "open" due to meaning representation. Corpus-based methods overcome this by deriving associations from actual language use.
(sources: src_028)

AI disruption requires a forward-looking approach to leverage its benefits while mitigating its risks, necessitating robust infrastructure and data utilization.

> To confront the AI disruption, Rangsit campus is geared to be a role model of AI City for the full activation of the use of data and physical availability.

### Prevention-First Approach to Societal Problems
For significant societal problems like plastic dependency, a prevention-first approach is superior to merely treating symptoms. This involves proactive elimination and systematic replacement of problematic elements rather than focusing solely on post-consumption management (e.g., reduce, reuse, recycle).

The "Zero-Plastic Model" exemplifies this by emphasizing prevention over treatment and introducing innovative technological solutions for alternatives.

> The model represents the deviation from the “reduce, reuse, recycle” framework, emphasizing prevention over treatment and introducing innovative technological solutions for plastic alternatives.
(sources: src_014)

### Word Ambiguity and Contextual Interpretation in NLP
A single word often conveys ambiguity and its true meaning must be interpreted within its specific context. This principle is fundamental for effective natural language processing and guides the application of techniques like word embedding to capture contextual meaning.

Word embedding techniques are used to provide word features that account for context when classifying text, such as types of complaints.

> I believe that one word usually conveys ambiguity and it has to be interpreted by its context.
(sources: src_002)

### Word Segmentation as Essential Preprocessing for Unsegmented Languages
For languages like Thai that lack explicit word delimiters, word segmentation is an essential preprocessing step in Natural Language Processing and Machine Translation systems to correctly identify words for further analysis.

Automated segmentation is necessary because Thai script does not explicitly mark word boundaries.

> Because Thai has no explicit marker to separate words from one another, we have to preprocess the corpus with word segmentation program.
(sources: src_029)

### Algorithm-Dataset Matching for Computational Efficiency
Computational efficiency can be significantly improved by selecting algorithms that are well-matched to the specific characteristics of a dataset, without compromising prediction results.

Research has shown that comparing different algorithms (e.g., Naïve Bayes, SVM, Deep Learning) and feature engineering techniques (e.g., PCA, Standard Deviation) demonstrates that tailoring the algorithm to the dataset reduces computation time while maintaining promising prediction accuracy.

> we can reduce the computation time by using the proper algorithm that matches with each dataset characteristics while all prediction results still promising.
(sources: src_003)

### Probabilistic Dictionary-less Approach for Search Engines
A probabilistic-based dictionary-less approach can be more effective for search engines than traditional word-based indexing, leading to a higher number of found and relevant documents.

Empirical evaluation supports the superiority of this method in terms of retrieval performance.

> Statistical testing shows that the dictionary-less approach is better than the word-based indexingapproach in terms of the number of found documents and the number of relevance documents.
(sources: src_002)

### Example-Based Learning for Meaning Acquisition
Understanding and conveying meaning, especially for complex or nuanced terms, can be effectively achieved through practical, example-based learning rather than solely relying on exhaustive formal definitions.

This approach is particularly useful in NLP for building resources like dictionaries, where sample sentences can illustrate meaning in practice.
