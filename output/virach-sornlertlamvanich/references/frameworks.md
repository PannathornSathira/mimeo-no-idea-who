## Frameworks of Virach Sornlertlamvanich

Virach Sornlertlamvanich has developed and utilized several frameworks to address complex problems in AI, NLP, and societal challenges. These frameworks provide structured approaches for designing systems, developing resources, and tackling real-world issues.

### Dual-Process Architecture for Large Language Models (LLMs)
A framework to enhance LLMs and mitigate their knowledge deficiencies by augmenting them with external knowledge and integrating semantically aware reasoning, inspired by human System 1 (fast, intuitive) and System 2 (slow, deliberate) cognition.

This architecture treats the base LLM as System 1 and the augmented external knowledge with reasoning as System 2, improving performance in tasks like FAQ retrieval by providing deeper cognitive capabilities.

> Inspired by the study of the human thought process, which is categorized into two systems reflecting the brain’s balancing act between speed and cognition, we propose a dual-process architecture that augments System 1 with additional knowledge akin to System 2 in human cognition.
(sources: src_014)

### Zero-Plastic Model
A comprehensive framework for addressing and eliminating plastic dependency in society by focusing on complete elimination and systematic replacement of plastic products, emphasizing prevention over treatment, and introducing innovative technological solutions.

This model represents a deviation from the traditional "reduce, reuse, recycle" framework, advocating for proactive measures to tackle environmental challenges.

> This paper proposes a “Zero-Plastic Model” that focuses on the complete elimination and systematic replacement of plastic products. The model represents the deviation from the “reduce, reuse, recycle” framework, emphasizing prevention over treatment and introducing innovative technological solutions for plastic alternatives.
(sources: src_014)

### Data Sensorium
A conceptual framework for systems that provide users with a physical, immersive experience of content stored in a database, primarily utilizing spatial immersive displays.

This framework aims to translate abstract database content into tangible, sensory experiences for users.

> Data Sensoriumis a conceptual framework of systems providing physical experience of content stored in database. Spatial immersive display is a key technology of Data Sensorium.
(sources: src_014)

### WordNet Management System (WNMS) for Cross-Language WordNet Development
A distributed management system and network for developing and disseminating cross-language WordNets, particularly for Asian languages, by mapping individual language WordNets to Princeton WordNet (PWN) and utilizing web service protocols for independent maintenance and interoperability.

Each node maintains a WordNet for a specific language, linked via PWN synset IDs. The system provides web service applications for editing, visualizing, and language processing, allowing direct API calls.

> We propose a language resource management system, called WordNet Management System (WNMS), as a distributed management system that allows the server to perform the cross language WordNet retrieval, including the fundamental web service applications for editing, visualizing and language processing.
(sources: src_002)

### Corpus-based Method for Noun Classifier Associations (NCA)
A corpus-based method to generate Noun Classifier Associations (NCA) from large corpora, recomposed under concept hierarchy and frequency constraints, to overcome challenges in classifier assignment and semantic construction of noun phrases in languages like Thai.

This method provides a statistical solution where rule-based approaches are insufficient due to the lack of exact rules and fluctuation in classifier selection.

> We propose a corpus-based method... which generates Noun Classifier Associations (NCA) to overcome the problems in classifier assignment and semantic construction of noun phrase. The NCA is created statistically from a large corpus and recomposed under concept hierarchy constraints and frequency of occurrences.
(sources: src_028)

### Knowledge Graph Construction from Text
A framework for extracting meaning and semantic relations from digitized text collections to build knowledge bases or knowledge maps, involving keyword identification, semantic relation discovery, triple creation, and information summarization.

Steps include human extraction of keywords, finding semantic relations, creating linked data triples, summarizing information, extracting symbols, creating info boxes for databases, and linking to knowledge maps.
(sources: src_010)

### Heuristic-based Thai Word Segmentation Program
A program for processing Thai language text that employs heuristic rules of longest matching and least word count, combined with character combining rules, to perform word segmentation and correct fault segmentation.

This is crucial for Thai, which lacks explicit word delimiters, enabling further NLP tasks like machine translation.

> The program employs heuristic rules of longest matching and least word count incorporated with character combining rules for Thai words.
(sources: src_029)

### I-D-A-R with B-L-P-A Framework for Digital Space Protection
A holistic, easy-to-understand framework (Idea-Dataset-Algorithm-Result with Brain-Learning-Planning-Action concept) for developing and implementing systems to protect digital spaces, addressing major concerns for developers.

This framework outlines all significant modules and steps for system development, from initial conceptualization to action.

> We present an effective holistic framework which is easy to understand, easy to follow, and easy to implement a system to protect our digital space in an initial state. This approach describes all steps with the significant modules (I-D-A-R: Idea-Dataset-Algorithm-Result Framework with B-L-P-A: Brain-Learning-Planning-Action concept) and explains all major concern issues for developers.
(sources: src_003)

### Conditional Random Field (CRF) Framework for Morphological Analysis of Unsegmented Languages
A framework for morphological analysis of unsegmented languages, such as Thai, formulated as a sequential supervised learning problem, where all possible word/tag segmentations are generated and an optimal path is selected using criteria like Viterbi score or confidence estimation.

This framework addresses the challenge of analyzing the internal structure of words in languages without explicit word boundaries.

> This paper presents a framework for Thai morphological analysis based on the theoretical background of conditional random fields. We formulate morphological analysis of an unsegmented language as the sequential supervised learning problem.
(sources: src_002)

### Asian WordNet Generation Algorithm (Translation-Based)
An algorithm for generating WordNets for Asian languages by translating synsets from an English base, disambiguating words by considering synonyms and English translations, and utilizing local English dictionaries.

This approach leverages the synonym-based definition of words in WordNet to facilitate cross-lingual resource creation.

> We proposed an algorithm that can disambiguate the word by considering the synonym, and its English translation. Asian WordNet (AWN) is therefore generated for many Asian languages by using each local English dictionary.
(sources: src_012)
